Support

Something not working, or an idea? Email support@douple.net with your phone model and what happened. We answer in English and Vietnamese.

Questions

  • My phone gets warm. Pause → set Graphics to Medium or Battery Saver, and switch Frame rate to 30 · Cooler.
  • The buttons are hard to reach. They sit where your thumbs rest in landscape; on iPad they scale with the screen. Pause → Look sensitivity changes how fast the view turns.
  • I got a new phone. On iPhone and iPad, sign in with the same Apple ID and iCloud, then open the game: the further-along save is restored. On Android, restore from your Google backup when setting up the phone.
  • I can't see the Watcher / the Hollow. That's the point. The Watcher only shows on camera, the Hollow only with the camera off.
  • Too many reminders? There are at most three after you leave, never at night. Turn them off in Settings → Notifications → DEADCAM.
  • I bought something on another phone. Open the Locker and tap Restore, signed in to the same App Store / Google Play account.
  • How do I change my ad consent? Locker → Ad privacy (shown where the consent rules apply).
  • How do I delete my data? See the Privacy Policy.

Hỗ trợ (Tiếng Việt)

Gặp lỗi hoặc có góp ý? Gửi email tới support@douple.net kèm mẫu điện thoại và mô tả sự cố. Máy nóng: Tạm dừng → Graphics chọn Medium hoặc Battery Saver, Frame rate chọn 30 · Cooler. Đổi máy: đăng nhập cùng Apple ID và iCloud (iPhone/iPad) hoặc khôi phục từ bản sao lưu Google (Android), tiến trình sẽ được lấy lại.
